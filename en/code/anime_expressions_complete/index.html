Facial expressions

Take a look at these facial expressions and try them in your own drawings.

To make your anime character look like they are in love, replace the eyes with two rounded hearts. Remember to add highlights to the corners to create a better effect.

To draw a happy expression, use curved closed eyes and a wide smile. Add soft blush marks to the cheeks to make the face look warm and cheerful.

To show an annoyed expression, angle the eyebrows downwards and narrow the eyes. A small frown or open shouting mouth helps the emotion stand out.
